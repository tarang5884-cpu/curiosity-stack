- 2-q miss on book / tonnes / cash = Kill.
- Theme must be Defence · Power · Tech-mfg · Biotech / energy materials.
- Flow is a cue. It does not promote a name onto Core.
- MOS_base SME = 35–40%. C×T×R capped 1.60. Peak-margin year uses blended OPM.
