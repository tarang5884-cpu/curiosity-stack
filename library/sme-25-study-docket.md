## 1. Why a docket, not a 25-stock PF

The source note is correct: SME edge is **process**, not a screenshot of 25 logos.

Most of these names already failed or stalled our MOS table at CMP (OBSCP, Millworks, CFF, Yash chase, Aimtron book vs dream, FlySBS). The docket exists so each name gets the **same file** before it can move to Live.

**Hard rules**
- Max **2 live SME** tickets at once (`sme-tracker.md`).
- Size **0.3–0.75%**. Circuit week = no add.
- Work FV must sit **≥25%** above intended add, or it is CAP — stalk only.
- 2 consecutive quarters miss on book / tonnes / cash = **kill**.
- Promoter pledge, RPT circus, or debtor days >150 without a contract reason = **do not open a file**.

## 2. The 10-pillar study (open a file only after 1–6 are filled)

| # | Pillar | What you write in the file | Fail =
|---|--------|----------------------------|--------|
| 1 | Concall vs prior promise | Guidance last year vs delivered | Serial miss |
| 2 | Annual report | Model, segments, RPT, contingencies | Related-party sales >15% unexplained |
| 3 | Quarter + deck | Sales, EBITDA, OCF, WC days, book | PAT up, OCF down 2 q |
| 4 | DRHP / RHP | Why IPO money; use of proceeds vs actual | Proceeds to promoter/loan repay only |
| 5 | Promoter | Holding, pledge, pay, dilution | Pledge or silent dilution |
| 6 | Exchange tape | Orders, capex, resignations | Order recycled / related party |
| 7 | BS + CF | Cash conversion, debt, capex mix | FCF never appears after “capex year” |
| 8 | SKU + customer | What is sold, to whom, switching cost | “AI / defence / export” caption only |
| 9 | Execution | Capacity funded vs hope | Book <0.5× sales + 3× sales target |
| 10 | Valuation + exit | Work FV, float, one-session exit | Cannot sell half without 8% hole |

Unseenvalue overlay on 7+9: **EBITDA → cash after WC → incremental ROIC on the next line.** Shilpa-style FCF inflection is rare here; assume capex is still rising.
