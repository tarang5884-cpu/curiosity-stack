## Layer Summaries

**L0 — Signal**
Global 2026 print is an AI-memory / advanced-packaging allocation spike (WSTS H1 +102% YoY; memory the engine). Binding constraint migrated from CoWoS to HBM. Same week, India notified Semicon 2.0 (₹1,27,500 Cr, six pillars). ISM 1.0 has three commercial Sanand lines (Micron ATMP, Kaynes OSAT, CG Semi OSAT). India still imports most silicon it consumes. Two maps must not be mixed: global rent is HBM/CoWoS/leading-node; India's live map is ATMP, power/legacy packages, design services, and a 28–110nm fab still ramping.

## Policy snapshot (31 Aug 2026)

Semicon 1.0 ~₹76,000 Cr, often 50% capex. Semicon 2.0 ₹1,27,500 Cr, differentiated: 40% silicon fab / 35% compound + advanced packaging / 30% equipment-materials / 25% legacy packaging. Design: strategic RfP + C-DAC co-own; commercial seed/equity/royalty; 9% net sales 5 years on new chips. R&D and talent up to 75% of project cost. Nodal: ISM / MeitY.

Six pillars: Design · Machines & materials · Fabs · ATMP/OSAT · R&D · Talent.
